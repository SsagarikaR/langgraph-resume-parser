Senior Backend Engineer (Python)
NimbusPay · Bengaluru, India (Hybrid) · Full-time · Experience: 5+ years

About the role
NimbusPay is hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to build the services behind our real-time payments platform. You will design high-throughput APIs, own services in production, and help us bring LLM-powered features into our risk and support workflows.

Responsibilities
- Design, build and operate Python microservices using FastAPI.
- Model data and tune queries in PostgreSQL; use Redis for caching and rate limiting.
- Build event-driven pipelines with Apache Kafka.
- Deploy and run services on Kubernetes in AWS; manage infrastructure with Terraform.
- Build LLM agent workflows with LangGraph / LangChain for internal automation.
- Mentor junior engineers and lead design reviews.

Required qualifications
- 5+ years of professional backend development experience.
- Strong Python skills, with production experience in FastAPI or Django.
- Solid experience with PostgreSQL and Redis.
- Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes.
- Experience with AWS (EC2, S3, RDS, EKS).
- Experience with message brokers such as Kafka or RabbitMQ.
- Good grasp of REST API design, testing (pytest) and CI/CD.

Nice to have
- Experience with LangGraph, LangChain or other LLM orchestration frameworks.
- Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code tools.
- Observability tools: Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry.
- Background in fintech or payments.

What we offer
Competitive salary and ESOPs, hybrid work (3 days in office), health insurance for you and your family, and a yearly learning budget.
